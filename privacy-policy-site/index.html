Official Privacy Policy
Application: Tenderly (Android / iOS)

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

🌱 1. Your Space, Your Privacy

Tenderly was built to be a calm, quiet, and private sanctuary for self-care and gentle reflection. We believe that caring for yourself should never come at the expense of your digital privacy.

Tenderly operates on an entirely local-first and offline-only architecture. The application does not connect to any proprietary servers, cloud databases, external APIs, or analytics platforms.

📦 2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data generated or entered while using Tenderly is stored exclusively within your device's local memory using React Native's AsyncStorage. No data is ever transmitted off your device.

Category Storage Key Purpose
Preferred Name @tenderly_user_settings Your preferred display name so Clover can address you warmly in greetings.
Clover Growth State @tenderly_clover_state Tracks Clover's current growth stage (1–5) and cumulative care count (0–21).
Daily Care Messages @tenderly_daily_messages_state Keeps daily care tasks and completed flags stable for the current calendar day.
Garden Sanctuary @tenderly_garden_flowers Stores the records of mature flowers gathered from completed growth cycles.
Custom Bouquets @tenderly_bouquets Stores bouquet arrangements created in the Bouquet Studio.
Whispers (Affirmations) @tenderly_whisper_state Tracks the randomized sequence index for daily affirmation display.
Visual Theme @tenderly_theme Saves your chosen color theme (Garden, Midnight, Lavender Dusk, Misty Morning).

🛡️ 3. What We Do NOT Collect

Because Tenderly is completely self-contained and operates without a remote server, we do not collect, process, or transmit any user data whatsoever. Specifically, there are:

  • No user accounts or logins: You never need to provide an email, phone number, password, or profile information.
  • No analytics or tracking: We do not track page views, button clicks, screen time, session lengths, or usage habits.
  • No advertisements: There are zero third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, or banner ads in the application.
  • No device identifiers or telemetry: We do not access your IP address, IMEI, Android Advertising ID (AAID), or hardware identifiers.
  • No location tracking: Tenderly does not access GPS, network location, or Wi-Fi data.
  • No external AI or chatbots: All plant messages and affirmations are bundled locally within static JSON files.
  • No in-app purchases or financial tracking: Tenderly has no subscriptions, paywalls, or payment processors.

🔄 4. How Your Data Is Used

The information saved on your device is used solely to maintain application state between sessions:

  • Allowing Clover to address you by your chosen name.
  • Remembering your plant's growth stage and care count.
  • Ensuring daily care requests reset naturally at local midnight.
  • Displaying your permanent sanctuary of gathered garden flowers and bouquets.
  • Preserving your selected visual aesthetic theme.

🤝 5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal information with any third party under any circumstances. There are no third-party software development kits (SDKs) embedded in Tenderly that collect or process user data.

🗑️ 6. Data Retention, Control & Deletion

You have full and unilateral control over all data stored by Tenderly:

Complete Deletion: Because all data is stored exclusively in your device's local application sandbox, deleting or uninstalling Tenderly from your device immediately, permanently, and irreversibly destroys all saved data, including your name, care history, garden blooms, and bouquets.

You can also reset data at any time without uninstalling by clearing the app's cache and storage in your Android/iOS system settings.

🧸 7. Children’s Privacy

Tenderly is designed for audiences of all ages, offering a gentle, peaceful botanical experience. Because Tenderly collects no personal information whatsoever, it fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy.

📱 8. Google Play Policy Compliance

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and Data Safety requirements:

  • Data Collection: No user data is collected.
  • Data Sharing: No user data is shared with third parties.
  • Security Practices: Because data is not transmitted over the internet, network transmission risks are inherently eliminated. Local device encryption provided by the operating system protects on-device application storage.

📝 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect future software enhancements or regulatory standards. If changes are made, the revised policy will be posted on this webpage with an updated Effective Date, and the in-app Privacy Policy screen will be updated in the corresponding app release.
