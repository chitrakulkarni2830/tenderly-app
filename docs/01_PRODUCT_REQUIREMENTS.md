# Product Requirements

## Product Purpose
Tenderly is a cozy, gentle self-care mobile application centered around a small plant named **Clover**. The core concept is: "take care of me to take care of you." The application is designed to feel soft, cozy, warm, comforting, peaceful, playful, slightly magical, and gender-neutral. 

## Target Experience
The app provides a calming, interactive experience where users interact with Clover through gentle care actions, listen to soothing ambient music, and read comforting "Light Whisper" messages.

## MVP Scope & Core Functionality
- **Clover Interactions:** Nurturing Clover through predefined care actions (Love, Water, Sunshine, Nourishment).
- **Growth Stages:** Clover progresses through distinct visual growth stages.
- **Light Whisper:** Displaying comforting, randomly selected messages.
- **Ambient Music:** A single, looping ambient music track playing in the background.
- **Settings & Personalization:** Capturing the user's name and displaying "About Tenderly" information.
- **Theming:** A choice of exactly four distinct themes (Garden, Midnight, Lavender Dusk, Misty Morning).
- **Offline Capable:** The entire MVP functions without an internet connection or backend.

## Functional vs. Visual Requirements
* **Functional Requirements:** Application logic, state management, persistence, audio playback, routing, and care action triggers. These will be implemented by the developer.
* **Visual/Design Requirements:** Colors, typography, layout, animations, Clover designs, icons, and theme specific visual assets. **These will be supplied in a future design handoff.** Developers must NOT invent or assume visual elements.

## Explicitly Excluded Features
The following features are **strictly excluded** from the MVP:
- Buy Me a Coffee (or any alternative monetization)
- Donations or payment processing (Razorpay, Stripe, etc.)
- Subscriptions or advertisements
- Affiliate links
- Login, signup, authentication, or backend APIs
- AI, chatbots, or generative content
- Journaling or habit tracking
- Streaks, points, coins, achievements, leaderboards
- Social profiles, social sharing, or virtual stores
- Multiple music tracks

*Note: There is no backend for this MVP. All data lives locally on the device.*
